      About this model

      This is a simplified diagram, not a copy of Bentham’s drawings. It has 96 cells in four tiers for illustration, and leaves out the galleries, stairs and roof he specified. Bentham used partitions inside the tower so no light showed through; here that is a solid dark core. No panopticon was built exactly as he planned, though later prisons such as Presidio Modelo in Cuba, built from 1926, borrowed the idea. Michel Foucault’s Discipline and Punish (1975) made it a symbol of power that works through the possibility of being watched.
