# Claude visual explainers

Interactive visual explainers of ideas from philosophy, maths and economics, built as single HTML pages with Claude.

## The explainers

- **[The panopticon – explore the model](https://hedgehoginthefogg.github.io/claude-visual-explainers/panopticon.html)**
  An interactive model of Bentham's prison design and the idea of power through constant, unseen observation.

- **[The rice and the chessboard – explorer](https://hedgehoginthefogg.github.io/claude-visual-explainers/rice-and-chessboard.html)**
  The old story of doubling grains of rice on each square, used to explore how quickly exponential growth gets out of hand.

- **[The tragedy of the commons – a riso film](https://hedgehoginthefogg.github.io/claude-visual-explainers/tragedy-of-the-commons.html)**
  A short animated film, in a risograph print style, about how shared resources get used up when everyone acts in their own interest.

## Viewing them

Click any link above to open it in your browser – nothing to install. Each explainer is a single self-contained HTML file, so you can also download one and open it offline.
